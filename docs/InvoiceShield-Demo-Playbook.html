How it works

From PDF to decision in seven steps

Rules find the problems. The model only reads fields and explains. A person makes every decision, and every step is logged.

1

Upload

PDF, PNG or JPEG. Type checked by magic bytes; SHA-256 hash stored.

2

Read

Text layer via unpdf, or Tesseract OCR for images.

3Local AI

Extract

Vendor, number, date, amounts, IBAN last 4. Numbers kept only if they appear in the document.

4

Check

Nine deterministic rules against the vendor register and invoice history.

5

Score

High 40 · Medium 18 · Low 6, capped at 100.

6Local AI

Explain

Plain-English summary and verification steps. Verdict words blocked.

7Human

Review

Mark reviewed, flag, clear, reopen, or correct fields. Append-only audit trail.

Score levels: Low 0–14Medium 15–39High 40–69Critical 70+

Rules find problems, AI doesn't

Every finding comes from a tested check and carries its evidence: this value against that value.

The model can't invent numbers

An amount from the model is rejected unless its digits are in the document. The parser value is used and the field is marked uncertain.

Nothing leaves the machine

With Ollama, invoice text stays local. With no model at all, the app runs on rules and template summaries.

Likely questions

QuestionAnswer
Where's the AI beyond a wrapper?The local model turns unstructured PDFs and scans into fields and writes the explanation, inside guardrails: numbers checked against the document, verdict words blocked, findings only from rules, rules-only fallback.
What's sovereign about it?Model runs locally on Ollama, files are on local disk, no external AI calls. Data store on the Firebase emulators on-prem, or the company's own project. Be precise if asked: the default config keeps auth and metadata in a Firebase project; emulator mode is fully local.
What data did you use?Entirely synthetic: fictional vendors and generated invoices, marked as synthetic on every PDF.
Is the score a fraud probability?No. It ranks what to review first, built from rule severities. The findings beside it are what matter.
What if extraction is wrong?Fields it couldn't read reliably are called out. The reviewer corrects them and re-runs checks; the correction is audited.
Scanned invoices?Images go through Tesseract OCR. A scanned PDF with no text layer is detected and the user is asked to upload an image.
Can it pay or block invoices?Deliberately not. It's a review step before your ERP's payment run.
How does it know a vendor's normal amount?A configured range per vendor, plus a robust z-score (median and MAD) against that vendor's history once there are 4+ invoices.
Teams and approvals?The prototype is per-user. Organisation workspaces, roles and ERP integration are on the roadmap.
